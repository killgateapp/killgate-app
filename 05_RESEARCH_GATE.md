# Research Qualification Gate

## Deterministic minimum gate

A `RESEARCH_PASS` is forbidden unless all minimums are met:

- contract was locked before evidence scoring;
- at least 3 independently useful DIRECT public sources;
- DIRECT public evidence spans at least 2 independent domains/source groups;
- DIRECT evidence of the target customer's problem/pain;
- DIRECT evidence of current behavior, workarounds, or alternatives;
- DIRECT evidence of budget, pricing, existing spend, or a credible spend proxy;
- disconfirming/negative evidence was actively searched;
- at least 2 material supporting claims are source-backed;
- at least 2 disconfirming/constraint findings are surfaced for a PASS-quality packet;
- no known fatal legal/platform/technical/trust constraint remains unresolved.

If source-content verification is available, use verified content rather than search snippets alone for a PASS.

## High-quality packet targets

For stronger confidence, aim for:

- ~30 relevant observed-user evidence items after deduplication;
- ~15 independent observed voices when identity/context permits;
- 3 independent communities/domains, unless the buyer is demonstrably concentrated;
- at least 3 recurring pain/workflow themes;
- at least 2 distinct current workarounds;
- at least 3 alternatives/pricing observations where competitors exist.

These are quality targets for a robust packet. Never fake quantity to hit them.

## Outcome rules

### RESEARCH_FAIL
Use when the hard relevance/sufficiency gate fails. Say **which gate failed**. Do not claim the idea is dead merely because search quality was poor.

### RESEARCH_PIVOT
Use when sufficient public evidence shows some real problem signal but the current ICP, offer, positioning, price, or economic basis is misaligned or incomplete.

### RESEARCH_PASS
Use only when all deterministic minimum gates clear. Cap confidence: a research pass is provisional because no direct buyer/payment evidence has been tested yet.

## Required scorecard fields

- direct source count;
- independent source-group/domain count;
- pain coverage;
- workaround/alternative coverage;
- budget/spend coverage;
- strongest supporting evidence;
- strongest disconfirming evidence;
- unresolved uncertainty;
- recommendation;
- confidence;
- exact human questions that remain unanswered.
