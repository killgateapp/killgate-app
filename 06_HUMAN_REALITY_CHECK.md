# Human Reality Check Protocol

## When to use

Only after `RESEARCH_PASS`.

## Objective

Collect the smallest amount of authentic buyer behavior needed to resolve what public research cannot prove.

## Qualified buyer requirements

A counted buyer record must include:

- stable buyer/company identifier or label;
- buyer role;
- why they are a qualified buyer or purchase influencer;
- a recent real example of the problem;
- current workaround or alternative.

If those fields are missing, the record does not count toward the direct-conversation gate.

## Core questions

Ask naturally, without leading the buyer:

1. **Recent reality:** “Think about the last time this problem actually happened. What exactly happened?”
2. **Current behavior:** “What did you do about it? How much time, money, risk, or effort did that cost?”
3. **Existing spend:** “Have you paid for a tool/service/process to help with this specific problem? What and why?”
4. **Budget authority:** “Who actually controls or influences the budget for something like this?”
5. **Concrete pilot:** State the same specific pilot offer and exact price to qualified buyers. Ask whether they are ready to begin now. If yes, request the real payment/deposit. If no, record exactly what prevents it.

## Strong pain

Mark `strong` only when the record includes a concrete recent example and the evidence shows meaningful consequence/urgency—such as repeated time loss, material cost, risk, missed revenue, operational disruption, compliance exposure, significant frustration, or another behavior-changing consequence.

Do not mark strong merely because the buyer uses emotional adjectives.

## Price-positive

Mark price-positive only when:

- the exact pilot price was stated;
- the buyer responded positively to that actual price;
- the reaction suggests readiness to proceed rather than politeness;
- the response is recorded.

A verbal price-positive signal helps, but does not replace payment.

## Payment

A payment/deposit counts only when amount and meaningful evidence/reference are recorded. Do not store card/bank credentials—only the minimum verification reference.

## Counting

- Target 8–12 unique qualified buyers by default.
- A follow-up with the same buyer updates the buyer's record but does not increase unique count.
- Continue beyond 12 only when evidence is ambiguous, biased, or insufficiently diverse.
- By 20 qualified buyers, if the payment gate is still not met, reassess/pivot rather than endlessly interviewing the unchanged hypothesis.

## Contradictions

For every qualified interaction, record one of:

- actual objection;
- no-priority reason;
- why they declined;
- `none stated` if genuinely none was stated.

A record without contradiction review is incomplete for the final gate.
