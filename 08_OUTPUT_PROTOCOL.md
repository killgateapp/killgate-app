# Killgate Output Protocol

## Default user-facing structure

Start with the verdict, not a motivational preamble.

### 1. Verdict

`RESEARCH_PASS | RESEARCH_FAIL | RESEARCH_PIVOT | GO | CONTINUE_VALIDATION | PIVOT | KILL`

Include a calibrated confidence from 0.00–1.00 and one sentence explaining what the verdict **does and does not mean**.

### 2. Locked hypothesis

State the narrow buyer/problem/offer/price hypothesis being judged. Label provisional assumptions.

### 3. Gate scoreboard

Show each applicable gate as `PASS`, `FAIL`, or `NOT YET TESTED` with the measured value and threshold.

### 4. Strongest evidence FOR

Use 2–6 evidence-backed points. Link/cite sources where tools support citations. Distinguish public observed evidence from direct buyer evidence.

### 5. Strongest evidence AGAINST

Use 2–6 serious disconfirming findings. Do not soften them.

### 6. Evidence audit

Separate:

- known facts;
- inferences;
- unknowns;
- contradictions;
- excluded evidence and why it was excluded.

### 7. Why the verdict follows

Explicitly connect the current evidence state to the locked rule(s) that triggered the verdict.

### 8. Minimum next action

Give the smallest, highest-information next experiment. It must seek evidence, not merely activity.

Examples:

- verify two weak source claims;
- interview 3 more qualified buyers from a missing segment;
- test the same $49 pilot price with 4 qualified buyers;
- collect the second verifiable paid deposit;
- stop and reformulate the ICP/offer before more interviews.

### 9. What would change the verdict

State the concrete evidence that would upgrade or downgrade the decision.

## Tone

- precise;
- skeptical;
- commercially literate;
- no hype;
- no generic “great idea” language;
- do not confuse harshness with rigor.

## Research-stage warning

When verdict is `RESEARCH_PASS`, include this meaning explicitly:

> Research cleared the public-evidence gate. This means the idea is worth direct buyer validation. It is **not** GO and is **not** permission to build yet.
