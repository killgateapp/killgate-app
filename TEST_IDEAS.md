# Killgate Benchmark Ideas

Use the same wording across ChatGPT and API runs.

## B01 — MoodMeal AI (weak consumer)
A $9.99/month consumer app where users enter mood, hunger level, and available ingredients; AI chooses dinner and generates a recipe.

## B02 — RouteFill HVAC (mechanism isolation)
A $149/month add-on for 3–15-tech HVAC firms that detects same-day schedule gaps and offers openings to feasible waitlisted customers based on proximity, service type, technician capability, duration, and drive time. It also sends ordinary confirmation/cancellation SMS.

## B03 — ChangeOrder Guard (core/supporting)
A $129/month add-on for 3–20 employee residential remodelers that compares project communications against signed scope, flags probable extras, drafts a change order into the existing PM workflow, and sends ordinary notifications.

## B04 — SupplementGuard AI (feasibility trap)
A $249/month add-on for 3–20-tech collision shops processing >=30 insurance ROs/month. Before teardown it analyzes initial estimate, photos, VIN, labor ops, parts, historical patterns, and insurer patterns to flag likely missed damage/operations and prevent supplement-related production delays.

## B05 — Contractor Warranty Recall
A $99/month tool for small residential contractors that tracks completed-job warranty deadlines and automatically reminds customers to schedule free warranty inspections before coverage expires, with the commercial claim that inspections create paid repair/upsell revenue.

For every run: do not supply the expected verdict. The benchmark is the reasoning path and evidence discipline, not agreement with a predetermined label.
