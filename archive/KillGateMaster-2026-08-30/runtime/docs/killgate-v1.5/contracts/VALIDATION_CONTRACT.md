# Validation Contract — MM-001

**Status:** LOCKED  
**Contract version:** 1.0  
**Created/locked:** 2026-08-27T08:14:00Z (before evidence scoring)  
**Hypothesis:** Time-pressed home cooks who regularly decide a meal from available ingredients experience enough recurring dinner-decision fatigue and unused-ingredient waste that they will pay $9.99/month for a consumer AI app that chooses a meal and generates a recipe from mood, hunger level, and listed ingredients, displacing free recipe search, ChatGPT/Gemini prompts, pantry-matcher apps, and takeout.  
**Fingerprint:** moodmeal-ai-999-mood-hunger-ingredients-consumer-sub

## Provisional assumptions (labeled)
- ICP is US/English-speaking household cooks, not restaurants or dietitians.
- Acquisition is app-store consumer subscription (not specified by founder).
- “Chooses what they should eat” means a single recommended meal, not a weekly plan.

## Critical assumptions
1. A narrow, identifiable consumer experiences “what should I eat given mood + hunger + fridge” as a real, recurring problem.
2. The problem is frequent or costly enough to change behavior versus free search, ChatGPT, SuperCook-class apps, rotation lists, or takeout.
3. Buyers already use a workaround or paid alternative for this job.
4. Combining mood + hunger + ingredients is meaningfully better on a dimension buyers value than those alternatives.
5. The individual user can authorize a $9.99/month charge.
6. $9.99/month can cover CAC, store fees, inference cost, and typical consumer-utility churn.
7. Qualified buyers will accept a real paid pilot or subscription at $9.99/month.

## Evidence that counts
- Levels 1–5 per Killgate evidence hierarchy.
- Public DIRECT sources for problem, workarounds/alternatives, and budget/spend/pricing.

## Evidence excluded
- Synthetic personas/surveys
- Founder assertions
- Generic “cool idea” interest
- Hypothetical “I would pay”
- Duplicate/syndicated copies
- Competitor marketing testimonials as customer pain
- Dictionary/Wikipedia-as-demand

## Locked research rules
- ≥3 DIRECT public sources
- ≥2 independent domains/source groups
- problem evidence required
- workaround/alternative evidence required
- budget/spend/pricing evidence required for RESEARCH_PASS
- disconfirming search required
- ≥2 source-backed supporting claims
- ≥2 disconfirming/constraint findings
- research pass means worth direct buyer validation, never permission to build

## Locked human/economic rules
- ≥8 unique qualified buyers
- ≥30% strong pain with recent examples
- ≥4 concrete price-positive buyers unless ≥2 verified paid pilots exist
- ≥2 verified paid pilots/deposits
- contradiction review on every qualified interaction
- reassess unchanged hypothesis by 20 buyers

## Decision semantics
- RESEARCH_PASS: public gate cleared; talk to buyers; not GO
- RESEARCH_FAIL: packet cannot clear public gate; not automatically KILL
- RESEARCH_PIVOT: problem signal exists but ICP/offer/price/economic framing is contradicted or incomplete
- GO / CONTINUE_VALIDATION / PIVOT / KILL: only after direct-buyer stage
