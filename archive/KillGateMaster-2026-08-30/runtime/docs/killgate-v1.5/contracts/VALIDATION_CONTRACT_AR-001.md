# Validation Contract — AR-001

**Status:** LOCKED  
**Contract version:** 1.0  
**Created/locked:** 2026-08-27T08:18:00Z (before evidence scoring)  
**Hypothesis:** Independent auto repair shop owners/managers lose enough booked-but-empty bay revenue from no-shows and short-notice cancellations that they will pay $99/month for a SaaS tool that auto-confirms appointments by SMS and backfills opened slots from a customer waitlist, displacing manual phone calls, generic calendar reminders, deposits-only policies, or reminder features already inside their shop management system.  
**Fingerprint:** indie-autoshop-99-sms-confirm-waitlist-backfill

## Provisional assumptions
- Buyer is the shop owner or service manager with card authority (not a dealer fixed-ops director).
- Shop already books appointments (not walk-in-only).
- Acquisition is outbound/sales to independent shops (founder unspecified).

## Critical assumptions
1. Independent shops experience material no-show/cancel losses in real operations.
2. The loss is frequent or costly enough to change behavior at $99/month.
3. Shops already use a workaround (calls, texts, SMS reminders, deposits, overbooking, walk-in days).
4. Standalone SMS confirm + waitlist backfill is meaningfully better than the reminder tools already in Shopmonkey/Tekmetric/AutoLeap/Podium.
5. The owner can authorize $99/month on top of existing shop software.
6. $99 can cover SMS/10DLC costs, integration, and sales CAC to fragmented shops.
7. Qualified buyers will pay a real $99 pilot.

## Locked research and human rules
Default Killgate numeric gates. Research pass ≠ permission to build. GO requires 8 qualified buyers, ≥30% strong pain, 4 price-positive or 2 paid pilots, 2 verified paid pilots, contradictions recorded.
