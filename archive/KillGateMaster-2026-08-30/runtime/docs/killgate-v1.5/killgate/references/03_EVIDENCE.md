# Evidence Rules

## Levels
0 Synthetic — proves nothing about demand
1 Market fact — pricing, regulation, product pages
2 Observed — public user/operator language and behavior
3 Direct — authentic qualified-buyer interaction
4 Commitment — costly action short of payment
5 Payment — completed payment or verifiable paid deposit

DIRECT public relevance ≠ Level 3.

## Never count as demand
AI personas; founder assertions; likes; “I would pay” with no offer; Wikipedia-as-demand; seller testimonials unlabeled; competitor copy as customer pain.

## Vendor datasets
Record owner, product sold, measurement definition, comparison groups, treatment standardization, inspectability, incentive to publish this finding. Sample size ≠ independence.

## Mechanism actions
- KEEP — material and supported or clearly plausible as infrastructure the buyer already values
- TEST — material and unproven. If the unproven claim is buyer behavior, human validation. If it is a load-bearing technical capability, feasibility test first (FEASIBILITY_REQUIRED), not $price interviews.
- Split stacked cores. “Detect A + predict B + therefore delay falls + therefore they pay $X” is four claims. Score them separately. Commodity-plausible A does not make speculative B a RESEARCH_PASS.
- REMOVE_DEFER — commodity, unnecessary, or bundled downstream infrastructure; dropping it does not change who pays / for what / at what price
- CONTRADICTED — evidence attacks a material value-producing claim

## Bundled differentiator vs bundled downstream
- Differentiator: the buyer would be paying you for a job the incumbent already sells as its product. That can force RESEARCH_PIVOT of the offer.
- Downstream infrastructure: an incumbent step (e-sign, calendar writeback, email) that the differentiated mechanism can use. Mark REMOVE_DEFER. Prefer “feed Buildertrend” over “own a second portal.”
- Question: does owning this component materially contribute to why they pay the locked price? If no, REMOVE_DEFER. If yes and it is already the incumbent’s product, RESEARCH_PIVOT.

## Source diversity vs independence
Diversity = different domains. Independence = unlinked incentives and data-generating processes. Three vendor help centers can prove a feature exists. They are one family for demand/WTP/revenue-lift claims.
