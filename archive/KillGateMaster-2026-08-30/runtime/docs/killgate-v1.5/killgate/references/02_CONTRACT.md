# Validation Contract

Lock immediately after hypothesis normalization and before scoring.

Record: version, hypothesis, fingerprint, critical assumptions, mechanisms (provisional core/supporting), what counts, what does not, research thresholds, human thresholds, economic thresholds, verdict semantics.

Default human/economic gates (frozen):
- 8 qualified unique buyers
- ≥30% strong pain with recent real examples
- 4 price-positive at the exact locked price unless 2 paid already exist
- 2 verified paid pilots (amount > 0 + meaningful payment reference)
- objections recorded on every qualified interaction
- reassess unchanged framing by 20 qualified buyers without 2 paid pilots

A proposed next test is not a contract. Before interview #1 on a new hypothesis, lock ICP, qualifying threshold, surviving mechanisms, integration assumption, exact price (same at checkout), strong-pain definition, qualified-buyer definition, default numeric gates, stop rule.

Implementation constraints from REMOVE_DEFER do not require a new contract if the locked WTP sentence is unchanged. Write the constraint into the existing contract as a lock, not a rewrite of buyer/problem/price.
