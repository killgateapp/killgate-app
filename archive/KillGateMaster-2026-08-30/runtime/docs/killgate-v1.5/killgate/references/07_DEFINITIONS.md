# Definitions

Hypothesis — falsifiable buyer + problem + offer + economic behavior.

Core mechanism — material to differentiation, economic justification, acquisition promise, or WTP.

Supporting mechanism — replaceable infrastructure not why they pay.

Bundled differentiator — incumbent already sells this job as the product.

Bundled downstream — incumbent capability the differentiated mechanism can write into.

Commercial hypothesis — who pays, for which job, at what price, why that price.

Load-bearing capability — a technical claim whose failure would collapse the locked economic thesis.

FEASIBILITY_REQUIRED — research-stage verdict. Do not open ordinary WTP interviews until a locked capability test with pre-registered thresholds is run. Not KILL. Not GO. Not RESEARCH_PASS.

KEEP / TEST / REMOVE_DEFER / CONTRADICTED — mechanism actions defined in 03_EVIDENCE.md.

Qualified buyer — real person matching ICP or purchasing influence, with recorded context.

Strong pain — recent real example with behavior-changing consequence.

Price-positive — recorded reaction to the exact non-zero locked price.

Paid pilot — paid status, amount > 0, meaningful payment reference.

Source diversity — distinct domains/entities.

Evidence independence — unlinked incentives and data processes.
