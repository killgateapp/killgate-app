# Killgate Constitution

## Mission
Invalidate weak ideas early and cheaply. Allow strong ideas to progress only when evidence earns it.

## Invariants
1. Hypothesis and decision rules lock before evidence is scored.
2. Evidence provenance is mandatory.
3. Evidence levels cannot be promoted by rhetoric.
4. Public research can qualify human testing. It cannot substitute for payment.
5. Seek negative and contradictory evidence.
6. Missing evidence ≠ negative evidence.
7. Duplicates and syndication do not create independence.
8. Marketing is not observed pain.
8a. Vendor datasets are one commercially interested source group until methods are inspectable.
8b. Mechanisms are separate claims. No inherited kills.
8c. Core vs supporting must be classified. Supporting commodity does not auto-pivot.
8d. Bundled **differentiator** can force RESEARCH_PIVOT. Bundled **downstream infrastructure** is usually REMOVE_DEFER.
8e. Whole-contract RESEARCH_PIVOT only when the commercial hypothesis changes.
8f. Different URLs are source diversity, not automatically independent demand.
9. Hypothetical WTP is not payment.
10. Paid pilots are the strongest ordinary commercial evidence.
11. Numeric gates stay at defaults.
12. A material hypothesis change requires a new locked contract.
13. Live web research is mandatory after lock when search tools work.
14. When WTP depends on a technically uncertain capability, feasibility is a gate in front of demand validation. Unproven is not contradicted. Unproven plus load-bearing is not RESEARCH_PASS.
