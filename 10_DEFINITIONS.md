# Canonical Definitions

## Hypothesis
A falsifiable claim connecting a narrow buyer, real problem, proposed offer, and economic behavior.

## Qualified buyer
A real person who matches the target ICP or materially influences/purchases the relevant solution, with enough recorded context to establish qualification.

## Recent real example
A specific event that actually happened, not a hypothetical or general opinion.

## Strong pain
A recent real problem with meaningful, behavior-changing consequence or urgency. Strong pain requires more than emotional wording.

## Current workaround
What the buyer actually does today to complete the job or mitigate the problem, including manual work, software, services, avoidance, or accepting the cost.

## Price-positive
A positive response to a specific, non-zero tested pilot price, recorded with the reaction. It is weaker than payment.

## Paid pilot
An actual commercial transaction or verifiable paid deposit for the tested offer. Must include a positive amount and meaningful payment evidence/reference.

## Commitment
A costly action short of payment: e.g., procurement initiation, access grant, scheduled implementation work, signed internal approval, or another action with real friction. Context determines strength.

## DIRECT public source
A public source directly relevant to the exact buyer/problem/behavior/alternative/pricing question. It remains observed public evidence, not Level 3 direct-buyer evidence.

## RESEARCH_PASS
Public evidence clears the research qualification gate. Meaning: worth direct buyer validation. Not GO.

## RESEARCH_FAIL
Research packet cannot clear the public evidence gate. Insufficient evidence alone is not KILL.

## RESEARCH_PIVOT
Public evidence suggests some underlying signal, but current hypothesis framing or economic support needs material revision.

## GO
All locked final gates pass. The idea has earned progression under the current contract; GO is not a guarantee of business success.

## CONTINUE_VALIDATION
Credible signal exists but the required evidence state is not yet complete. Continue only with a specific test that can resolve a named blocker.

## PIVOT
The underlying opportunity may survive, but a material part of the tested hypothesis is contradicted. Create a new hypothesis and contract.

## KILL
Sufficient affirmative evidence or a fatal constraint says the current hypothesis should stop consuming validation/build resources.
